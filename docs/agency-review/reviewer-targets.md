## Exact invitation draft

This draft is ready for the user's approval. The attachment must be the final non-confidential sample packet. Do not attach raw provider responses or a private project URL. The public homepage is context only and does not share the owner's saved project.

Subject: Five-minute review of a campaign research sample?

Hi team,

I am building PitchProof for small creative agencies. It turns a brief and confirmed reference brands into campaign directions with links to the cultural research behind them.

Could a strategist or creative director spend five minutes reviewing the attached sample packet? It is an uncommissioned Nike Pegasus 41 exercise. Nike is not a client and has not endorsed it.

I would value three short answers. Which direction would you choose, and why? Which would you reject, and why? What evidence or missing information would change that decision?

The cultural links are research hypotheses. They do not prove audience preferences or grant advertising rights. No account or client data is needed for this packet review. The app is at https://pitchproof-studio.zippy17.chatgpt.site.

I am asking for candid private feedback. I would seek separate permission before quoting you or naming your agency publicly.

Thanks,
Vasu

## What this review can establish

A response can establish the reviewer's opinion about the sample and its evidence. It cannot establish that the reviewer can complete the live app workflow. A later hands-on review follows [the review protocol](review-protocol.md), including the initial direction, a rejected option, a revision after feedback, and the final export. Record the reviewer's exact words in [the worksheet](review-worksheet.md).

The [existing longer invitation](outreach-draft.md) describes that separate hands-on session. Do not claim its roughly 25-minute workflow test fits this five-minute packet review. Do not discuss an agreed paid pilot until commercial usage and export rights have been verified.
