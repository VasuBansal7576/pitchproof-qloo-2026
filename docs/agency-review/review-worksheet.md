## Record the reviewer's answers

1. Which decision changed after seeing the evidence? Name the observation and the change.
2. Which option did you reject, and why?
3. Which interpretation was useful, and which was unsupported?
4. Did every product claim follow from the supplied facts? Identify any correction.
5. Did the revision follow your feedback and preserve earlier history?
6. Could you distinguish fresh research from previous evidence?
7. Did the final export retain the notes and evidence needed for a client discussion?
8. Would you bring another permitted brief? State the situation and reason.
9. What would prevent repeat use?
