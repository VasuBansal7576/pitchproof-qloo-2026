# Run an agency review

Test whether the full PitchProof workflow helps an independent creative agency choose and explain a lifestyle campaign direction. Footwear is the first case. No external review or pilot commitment is recorded.

1. Use a hypothetical brief unless the reviewer has permission to supply client information.
2. Record the tested version, browser, role, and permitted brief in [the worksheet](review-worksheet.md).
3. Ask the reviewer to record a direction before opening PitchProof.
4. Start the task clock when the reviewer begins entering the brief.
5. Let the reviewer complete the workflow without taking over their decisions.
6. Ask the reviewer to inspect an exact observation behind a strong and a weak proposal.
7. Ask the reviewer to reject one option with a reason.
8. Ask the reviewer to give feedback and assess the resulting fresh research and revision.
9. Record each correction, its reason, and the hands-on correction time.
10. Ask the reviewer to add production notes and choose a direction.
11. Ask the reviewer to record operator acceptance only if the current proposal is suitable for further planning.
12. Verify the actual selected export with its notes and evidence.
13. Stop the task clock at the accepted export or the exact failure point.
14. Complete the worksheet in the reviewer's own words.

Count provider waits in elapsed task time and separately record hands-on time. Preserve incomplete runs. Automated readiness, internal semantic review, and operator planning acceptance are different outcomes.

If comparing with a normal agency method or an LLM-only workflow, use the same brief and acceptance rubric. Record order and prior exposure. One person's sequence is exploratory evidence, not a controlled time-saving result.

Obtain separate permission before quoting or naming a participant publicly. Keep repeat-use interest, a request for another brief, an agreed pilot, and actual payment as separate records. Discuss paid scope only after commercial use, export, retention, and asset rights are resolved. This protocol sends no invitation, starts no ad test, and enables no billing.
