## Prizes and additional investment

The cash pool totals USD 25,000, with USD 15,000, USD 6,000, and USD 4,000 prizes. The overview separately announces Jason Calacanis's additional USD 25,000 investment in his favorite entry. It gives no valuation, equity, or instrument terms. Neither selection nor investment is guaranteed. [Official overview](https://qloo.devpost.com/).

## Judging and rights

The four equally weighted criteria cover technological implementation, design, potential impact, and idea quality. Viability and API fit precede that scoring. The entrant remains responsible for eligibility and rights. [Official rules](https://qloo.devpost.com/rules).

Qloo's public terms incorporate service-specific terms and limit resale or redistribution. They do not establish this project's production price or client-export permission. Verify the issued-access agreement before paid use. [Qloo terms](https://www.qloo.com/legal/terms).
