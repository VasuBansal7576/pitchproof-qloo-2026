## Project name

PitchProof

## Tagline

Campaign directions with traceable cultural research and an explicit agency decision.

## What it does

PitchProof is built for independent creative agencies preparing lifestyle campaign pitches. Footwear is the first case. The intended workflow covers research, creative comparison, feedback, production decisions, and an editable selected brief.

An agency enters its brief, a separate ledger of supplied product facts, a shared call to action, and three reference brands. It confirms each exact Qloo identity before research. The intended output is three distinct campaign proposals with concrete ad executions and links to their saved observations. Agencies can reject an option, request fresh research for a revision, add notes, select a direction, and export it. Generated proposals require human review.

## Why Qloo matters

Qloo resolves reference identities and supplies cross-category associations. The app retains each observation's query context and maps generated citation labels to canonical saved records. Revision research uses the feedback and creates a separate evidence set.

The creative interpretation remains a hypothesis. Reference roles do not establish actual customer preferences. Associations do not establish product benefits, endorsements, purchase intent, or campaign effectiveness. Place-query geography does not apply to every observation.

Historical reference comparisons showed different evidence and proposals when only the references changed. That is evidence of reference sensitivity in those runs. A controlled LLM-only comparison and superior campaign results remain unproven.

## What we built

The app uses React, Vinext, Cloudflare D1, Qloo, and Gemini. It saves completed research during interruptions. It validates product claims, citations, and structured proposals before publication. A separate automated review checks the proposal set. Private validated drafts support review-only retries when their context remains valid.

The server owns the concept-pilot contract. The model supplies one declarative campaign message. Both arms share the base edit, shot order, soundtrack, selected fact set, 15-second duration, channel, audience, exact call to action, and landing page. Only message text changes. Proposed spend and delivery floors require agency decisions. They establish neither permission to launch nor statistical significance.

Operator acceptance records approval for further planning against the current project version. It does not approve an ad launch, asset rights, spending, or campaign performance.

## Who it is for

The intended buyer is an independent agency that needs to explain why a proposed campaign follows from its research. No external agency review, customer traction, measured time saving, paid commitment, or campaign result is established. The first review will measure time to an accepted export, correction effort, and interest in another permitted brief.
