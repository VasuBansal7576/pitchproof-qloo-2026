# Founder pitch and questions

Draft briefing. Customer demand and current complete live acceptance remain unverified.

## Spoken pitch

PitchProof helps independent creative agencies turn lifestyle briefs into campaign proposals they can question and explain. Footwear is our first case. An agency supplies product facts and confirms reference brands. Qloo supplies cultural associations. Each proposal connects a concrete creative interpretation to its exact saved observations and states what those observations cannot prove.

The agency can reject an option, give feedback for fresh research, preserve earlier versions, add production decisions, and export a selected brief. A server-owned concept pilot changes only the message while holding shared assets and controls fixed. Operator acceptance records readiness for further planning.

We have verified live Qloo research with 54 records on the current release. Current creative generation is blocked by claim validation, schema failures, and provider availability. We have no external agency acceptance or revenue evidence yet. Our next milestones are a repeatable complete live journey, observed agency use with measured corrections and elapsed time, and clear commercial rights and cost per accepted brief.

## Questions to answer candidly

| --- | --- |
| Who buys this? | Independent creative agencies preparing lifestyle campaign pitches are the intended buyer. The budget owner and buying process require interviews. |
| Why Qloo rather than a generic LLM? | Qloo provides saved cross-category observations and exact identity context. Current research works. Better creative decisions require a controlled comparison and external review. |
| What traction do you have? | No external agency review, paid commitment, or repeat-use evidence is recorded. Internal samples are not customer traction. |
| How do you acquire agencies? | Proposed first channel is founder-led reviews of permitted briefs, followed by consented pilot discussions. No conversion rate or scalable acquisition cost is established. |
| What brings an agency back? | The hypothesis is a new client brief or a feedback round. Measure a request for another permitted brief separately from polite interest. |
| What is defensible? | The current implementation combines provenance, explicit fact limits, revision history, and agency decisions. A durable competitive advantage remains unproven. |
| How do you make money? | A paid brief or agency subscription is a hypothesis. Price, demand, rights, and support burden remain unknown. Use [the conditional economics](unit-economics.md) before a paid offer. |
| What can fail? | Provider capacity, unsupported generated claims, weak interpretation, unresolved client-export rights, or correction work that exceeds the value. Preserve the failure record. |
| What would investment buy? | Proposed milestones are provider reliability, a small observed agency cohort, rights and pricing confirmation, and evidence of repeat use. Set an actual budget after quotes and scope. |
| Is USD 25,000 promised? | No. The announced favorite-entry investment is separate from the USD 25,000 cash-prize pool. Terms and selection are unspecified. See [the official requirements](official-requirements.md). |

The broader goal remains a full agency workflow across lifestyle campaigns. The footwear case tests that goal without claiming other categories are validated.
